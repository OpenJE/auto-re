CHANGES

1. autore-stage1/src/scheduler/scheduler.rs:
   - Replaced `RepositorySet` argument with `Arc<dyn AutoReClient>` + `ProjectId` + task snapshot in `run_campaign` and related methods.

DESIGN NOTES
------------
- Snapshot-based design: the caller provides a task snapshot; the scheduler makes decisions and issues commands. The snapshot does NOT update after mutations within the same tick.
- `dispatch_tasks` considers both `Ready` and `Pending` tasks with satisfied dependencies, since promotion commands have been issued but the snapshot still shows `Pending`.
- `evaluate_state` checks terminal-first (Complete takes precedence over dispatched count).
- Production code: 283 pure LOC (SIZE_OK — single responsibility: scoring + evaluation).
