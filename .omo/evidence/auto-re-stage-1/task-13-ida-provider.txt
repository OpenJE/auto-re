DESIGN DECISIONS
-----------------
- idax is an optional dependency behind "ida" feature flag. Without the feature,
  ida.binary.open returns a Diagnostic{code=ida.feature.disabled} + Completed{Failed}.
  This allows building the provider without the IDA SDK installed.
- Event construction uses a compact Ctx tuple + evt! macro to stay under the 250 LOC
  ceiling in provider.rs (236 pure LOC with 9 capabilities).
- Artifact staging uses std::env::temp_dir()/ida-provider-staging/<request_id>/ —
  paths do NOT contain "artifacts/sha256" (verified by test).
- All ExecutionEvents include provider_instance_id, request_id, operation_id,
  capability_id, capability_version, and monotonic sequence numbers.
