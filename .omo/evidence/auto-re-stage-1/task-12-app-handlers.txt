## Key Finding: MutexGuard Deadlock
- Holding Database::connection() MutexGuard while calling events_after() causes
  deadlock because LocalProjectEventService also acquires the same DB mutex.
- Fix: scope all conn MutexGuards in blocks, releasing before calling event service.
